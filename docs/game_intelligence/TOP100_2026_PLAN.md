# GI-07 — 2026 mobile top-100 research and PC reference games

## Scope
Expand the research corpus to the top 100 **eligible mobile games per genre** for each of the 12 genre families in `genres.json`, using Google Play and Apple App Store 2026 chart snapshots, and a separate PC reference corpus. Target 1,200 genre-game memberships, not necessarily 1,200 unique titles. Do not fabricate ranks or pad sparse genres with unrelated games.

## Definitions and provenance
- Track store, country, chart type (free/paid/grossing), observation date/time, source URL, original store category, app ID, game title, publisher, platform, position, and genre tags.
- Google Play and App Store publish distinct country/store charts. No single official global top-100 by our custom genre taxonomy. A derived worldwide ranking must be clearly marked as an estimate and show its methodology.
- Prefer 2026 historical snapshots across months, not just a single-day list. Compute an explicitly labeled `2026 popularity index` from available observations, keeping free, paid and grossing as separate dimensions. Never label an estimate as an official chart.
- Genres MMORPG, ARPG, 4X, economic strategy etc. are editorial classifications, not necessarily store categories. Apply genre mapping after collecting chart data, with reviewable evidence and confidence.
- If a niche genre has fewer than 100 chart-verified titles, report the actual number and supplement with a separately labeled discovery list. Never fabricate a top 100.
- Regional focus: US, RU where storefront data is accessible, DE, GB, JP, KR, IN, BR; additional markets when sources permit. Preserve regional differences.
- Do not assume app downloads or revenue from chart positions alone.
- Collection must respect source terms, licensing, request limits and privacy. Keep citations/URLs and short summaries, not bulk copyrighted reviews.

## Mandatory PC reference
Capitalism Lab (https://www.capitalismlab.com/) is a PC business simulation reference, NOT a mobile top-chart entry. Study manufacturing/retail, supply chains, pricing, branding, R&D, subsidiaries, city economics, competition and public companies. Compare with Capitalism II, Anno 1800, Victoria 3, Software Inc., GearCity, Transport Fever 2. Add other verified genre references as relevant.

## Player sentiment curriculum
For each prioritized game: at least one verified source for a mechanics overview and a balanced review dossier when sources exist. Capture:
- what players praise (core loop, agency, progression, co-op, UI, fairness);
- what players criticize (grind, pay-to-win, intrusive timers, opaque economy, bugs, performance, bad onboarding);
- evidence source type (store review, Reddit discussion, forum, professional review), date, audience bias, sample size when known;
- recurring themes, contradictory opinions, confidence, and applicability to Android/Godot;
- design lessons with evidence IDs and tradeoffs, never generic claims presented as research.
Do not treat developer testimonials or marketing copy as representative player consensus. Store public review excerpts only when needed and short; anonymize commenters.

## Suggested structure
`research/2026/sources.json` provenance registry
`research/2026/charts/<store>/<country>/<date>.json` snapshots
`research/2026/genres/<genre_id>.json` editorial top lists with score and evidence
`research/games/<app_or_reference_id>/mechanics.json`
`research/games/<app_or_reference_id>/sentiment.json`
`research/pc_references/capitalism_lab.md`
`research/2026/METHODOLOGY.md` ranking and gaps
Avoid committing large unlicensed raw scraped reviews; store references and concise findings.
